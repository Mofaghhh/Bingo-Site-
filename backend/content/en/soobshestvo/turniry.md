# Tournaments

{% hint style="info" %}
The official tournaments section is available on the [Bingo Ranked Tournaments](/tournaments/) page.
{% endhint %}

Bingo Ranked tournaments are official competitive events on identical world seeds with strictly fair starting conditions.

---

### Tournament Paths

Competitions in Bingo Ranked are divided into two brackets:

#### 1. Content Creator Tournament
* **Target Audience:** Twitch and YouTube streamers, video creators.
* **Bracket:** 256 players, 1v1 elimination.
* **Prize Pool:** 20,000 RUB and exclusive medals.

#### 2. Community Tournament
* **Target Audience:** All registered players from Copper to Netherite tiers.
* **Bracket:** 256 players, 1v1 elimination.
* **Format:** Open participation (no prize pool or medals).

---

### Competition Format: 1v1 Speedrun Duel

Tournament matches are played in a head-to-head duel format:

* **Players:** 1v1;
* **Victory Condition:** First to complete any 5-objective line (row, column, or diagonal);
* **Time Limit:** 20 minutes. If no line is completed, the player with the most claimed objectives wins.

---

### Fair Play & Regulations

1. **Identical Seed:** Every contestant starts on the exact same world seed and initial spawn coordinates.
2. **Client Verification:** All clients are checked for prohibited modifications such as X-Ray, auto-clickers, and camera utilities. See the [banned modifications list](/docs/soobshestvo/zapreshyonnye-modifikacii/).
3. **Referee Decisions:** Official referee decisions regarding technical issues or disputes are final.

For detailed conduct requirements, visit the [Community Rules](/docs/soobshestvo/pravila) page.
