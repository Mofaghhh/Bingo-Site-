# Rules

Fair play keeps ranked matches clear and competitive for everyone.

1. Do not use [banned modifications](/docs/soobshestvo/zapreshyonnye-modifikacii).
2. Do not use automation.
3. Do not seek an unfair advantage.
4. Do not disrupt a running match.
5. Follow the decisions of tournament organisers.
6. Check the current list of banned modifications before a tournament.

Organisers may refine these rules.
