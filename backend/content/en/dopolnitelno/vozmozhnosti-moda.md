# Mod features

* **Bingo 5x5** — a board with 25 objectives.
* **Ranked** — competitive matches with ELO rating and ranks.
* **Matchmaking and Lobby** — opponent search and a Ready check before the match.
* **Matches** — the same seed, board, world generation and starting point.
* **Match and ELO history** — track your ranked results.
* **Seasons and leaderboard** — seasonal progress and rating comparison.
* **Friends and Messenger** — the social side of the mod.
* **Practice** — learn Bingo without ranked competition.

The Wiki navigation covers each mode and section in detail.
