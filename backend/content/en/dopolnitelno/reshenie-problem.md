# Troubleshooting

### The match takes a long time to start

Make sure both players loaded the world and pressed Ready. The match starts once both are ready.

### No opponent appears

Check your connection and wait for Matchmaking to finish. If the problem repeats, restart Minecraft.

### Ready does not register

Make sure the world has fully loaded. Check your connection and ask your opponent to confirm Ready.

### The world loads slowly

Check your connection. If loading does not finish, restart Minecraft and try again.

### A player disconnected

A disconnect or exit is handled by the server check. Do not leave the match yourself.

### Connection problems

Check your internet access. Restart Minecraft if the connection does not recover.

### The Wiki or site renders incorrectly

Reload the page. Check your connection and open the Wiki in another browser.

{% hint style="info" %}
If the problem involves the mod, check `latest.log` and report it to the developers.
{% endhint %}
