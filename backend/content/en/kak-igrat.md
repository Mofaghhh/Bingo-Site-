# How to Play Bingo Ranked: Beginner's Guide

Bingo Ranked is a competitive Minecraft mod that turns classic survival into a high-speed intellectual duel.

Two players connect to the same match, receive an identical 5×5 card with 25 objectives, spawn at the exact same location in a world with a shared seed, and race for speed. There is no random seed advantage: victory goes to the player with superior knowledge of crafting, biome generation, and routing efficiency.

---

## 1. Main Objective

Your goal is to be the first to complete any continuous line of **5 objectives** on the bingo card:
* Horizontally (any of the 5 rows)
* Vertically (any of the 5 columns)
* Diagonally (corner to corner)

As soon as you complete the fifth objective in a line, the match instantly concludes with your victory!

---

## 2. Solo Practice Mode

If you just installed the mod or have never played Minecraft Bingo, start with **Practice mode**:
1. Open the Minecraft main menu.
2. Click **Practice**.
3. A world will generate without a timer and without an opponent.

In practice mode, you can freely explore the card interface, practice your early-game opening (wood -> stone tools -> cave diving), and see how the objective detection system works.

---

## 3. The 5×5 Bingo Board

The interactive bingo board is displayed directly in-game as a 5×5 grid.

* **Card Hotkey:** `B` by default (configurable in Key Binds).
* **25 Random Objectives:** generated before each match starts.
* **Objective Categories:**
  * **Items:** obtain a rare or specific item (e.g. colored wool, flint, lava bucket, melon slice).
  * **Blocks:** mine or craft a block (piston, dripstone, smooth stone, chiseled sandstone).
  * **Advancements:** trigger an advancement ("Hot Stuff", "Monster Hunter", "Sniper Duel").
  * **Exploration:** visit a specific biome or find a structure.

### Objective Tracking

* As soon as a required item enters your inventory or an advancement triggers, the cell on your card turns **green** immediately.
* You do **not** need to keep holding or throw the item: it stays in your inventory and the goal is marked complete permanently.
* Your opponent receives an audio cue (bell ring) whenever you secure a cell. You also see the opponent's progress in real-time.

## 4. Step-by-Step Match Flow

{% stepper %}
{% step %}

### Select Mode in Main Menu

In the main menu, click **Bingo Ranked**:
* **Matchmaking:** automated queue matching you with an opponent of similar ELO rank.
* **Custom Lobby:** create a private room with a 6-digit code for friendly matches.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}

### World Generation & Chunk Loading

Both players connect to the game server. The server generates the world:
* The world seed is completely identical for both participants.
* The spawn coordinates match down to the millimeter.
* Surrounding structures, ore deposits, and biomes are in identical locations.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}

### Ready Confirmation

Once the world has loaded, press **Ready** in the center of the screen.
The match starts only when **both players** click Ready. A 5-second countdown then begins.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}

### Start & Route Planning

Restrictions lift and the timer starts. Open the board (`B`), quickly scout the fastest 5-objective line, and plan your gathering route:
1. Basic tool set (wood, crafting table, stone pickaxe).
2. Food and furnace for cooking.
3. Scout biomes around spawn (ocean, desert, village, dark forest).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}

### Completing the Line & Victory

Watch the HUD: if the opponent starts claiming cells on your target diagonal, accelerate or pivot to an adjacent vertical column. Once 5 consecutive cells are completed, victory is claimed, match time is recorded, and ELO points are awarded.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## 5. Surrender & Disconnects

* **Surrender:** if you are trapped in a cave, lost all items, or notice the opponent is far ahead, press `Esc` and choose **Surrender**. The match ends cleanly and you can immediately queue up again.
* **Forfeiting / Quitting:** closing the client or leaving to the title screen during an active match results in an ELO penalty equivalent to a defeat.
* **Opponent Disconnect:** do not leave the game! The server waits 120 seconds for the opponent to reconnect. If they do not return, you are awarded an automatic technical victory.

---

## 6. Beginner FAQ

### Can players fight each other (PvP)?
In standard 1v1 ranked duels, players focus purely on speedrunning objectives and routing. Direct player-versus-player damage is disabled to prevent unfair early spawn-killing.

### What if a required biome is not nearby?
The bingo generator balances objectives so goals can always be achieved within 1,000–1,500 blocks from spawn. If a line requires an elusive biome, look at parallel rows or columns: there is almost always an alternative line available.

### Is an official Minecraft account required?
Yes, competitive ranked play and official Bingo Ranked tournaments require authenticated official Minecraft accounts. Unlicensed / cracked launchers are blocked by security validation.
