# FAQ

### Why does the match not start immediately?

Both players must load the world and press Ready. The countdown starts after that.

### Why do we have the same board?

The same board, seed, world generation and starting point keep the conditions fair.

### What happens on surrender?

The player who surrenders takes the loss. The opponent is awarded the win automatically.

### Where do I check allowed modifications?

Review the [list of banned modifications](/docs/soobshestvo/zapreshyonnye-modifikacii) before a match or a tournament.

### Where do I get technical help?

See [Troubleshooting](/docs/dopolnitelno/reshenie-problem). If the mod errors, keep `latest.log`.
