# ELO Rating, Ranks & Tournaments

The competitive system in Bingo Ranked is based on the globally recognized ELO rating mathematics and periodic single-elimination tournament championships.

---

## 1. How ELO Rating Works

After each completed match, the rating of both competitors is updated automatically:
* **Victory against a higher-ranked player:** yields the highest point gain (+25...35 ELO).
* **Victory against an equally rated opponent:** yields standard points (+16...20 ELO).
* **Victory against a low-tier novice:** yields a modest increase (+8...12 ELO).
* **Defeat:** deducts points proportionally to the opponent's strength. Losing to a veteran master costs very little, while an unexpected upset against a beginner results in a maximum deduction.

### Placement Calibration

Every new player begins with a placement calibration phase (first 5 matches). Rating adjustments are amplified during calibration so you can quickly settle into an appropriate competitive bracket.

## 2. Leagues & Tier Divisions

Bingo Ranked features 6 competitive tiers inspired by Minecraft materials:

| Rank | ELO Range | Skill Profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Copper** | 0 – 799 ELO | Entry tier for beginners. Exploring UI, basic craft routines, and first victories. |
| **Iron** | 800 – 1199 ELO | Confident survivalists. Mastery over common recipes and biome routing. |
| **Gold** | 1200 – 1499 ELO | Experienced players. Optimized resource routes, rapid cave exploration, and structure scouting. |
| **Diamond** | 1500 – 1799 ELO | High competitive mastery. Completing difficult diagonals within minutes with strict clock management. |
| **Emerald** | 1800 – 2099 ELO | Semi-pro players and tournament veterans. Mastery over niche mechanics and fast crafting. |
| **Netherite** | 2100+ ELO | Elite server bracket. Top 100 speedrunners and Bingo champions. |

## 3. Seasons & Leaderboard

* **Season Length:** competitive seasons typically run for 2 to 3 months.
* **Leaderboard:** viewable directly in the mod's main menu and on the official website. It highlights player nicknames, current ELO, total wins, and win rate.
* **Seasonal Rewards:** at the conclusion of each season, players receive profile badges, custom avatar cosmetic frames, and community recognition based on their peak rank.
* **Soft Reset:** at the beginning of each new season, ratings undergo a soft reset towards the median value to allow everyone a fresh climb to the top.

---

## 4. Official 128-Slot Tournaments

Alongside daily matchmaking, the platform organizes premier tournament events:
* **Format:** Single Elimination bracket spanning **128 player slots**.
* **Bracket Structure:**
  * **Bracket 1 (Slots 1–64):** first half of the tournament tree from Round of 64 through the pool final.
  * **Bracket 2 (Slots 65–128):** second half of the tournament tree from Round of 64 through the pool final.
  * **Final Stage (Top 16):** the final 16 contenders battle for the championship crown.
* **Chosen Pairs:** participants who agreed to duel a specific rival during tournament registration receive seeded matchups and face each other in the opening round.
* **Random Matchmaking:** remaining participants are drawn into matches through automated lottery seeding.

Follow the current participant roster, seedings, and match progress on the [Tournaments](/tournaments/) page.
