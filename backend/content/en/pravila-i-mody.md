# Rules & Allowed Modifications

Bingo Ranked is an esport discipline where victory is determined solely by player skill: crafting knowledge, spatial navigation in Minecraft worlds, and tactical routing speed. The use of third-party modifications that grant an unfair informational or resource advantage is strictly prohibited.

---

## 1. Fair Play Code of Conduct

* **Official Licensed Account:** participation in ranked queues and official tournaments requires an authenticated Mojang / Microsoft account.
* **Cheats & Hack Clients Banned:** any use of X-Ray (mods or resource packs), Freecam, Baritone, Fly, KillAura, auto-clickers, and macros results in a permanent ban.
* **No Collusion / Win Trading:** intentional match throwing, arranged victories, or account sharing is prohibited. Offenders forfeit all season ranking and tournament eligibility.

---

## 2. Allowed Modifications (Fabric Optimization Whitelist)

To ensure high frame rates and stutter-free gameplay, the following client-side optimization mods for the Fabric loader are officially permitted:

| Mod | Purpose | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sodium / Embeddium** | Modern graphics rendering engine, massive FPS boost | **Allowed** |
| **Lithium** | Physics and game tick optimizations | **Allowed** |
| **FerriteCore** | Drastic reduction in client RAM consumption | **Allowed** |
| **ImmediatelyFast** | Accelerated rendering of text, HUD, signs, and UI elements | **Allowed** |
| **Entity Culling** | Skips rendering entities behind solid walls (purely FPS optimization, no ESP) | **Allowed** |
| **Memory Leak Fix** | Eliminates memory leaks during prolonged gaming sessions | **Allowed** |
| **Krypton** | Network stack optimization for Minecraft packet handling | **Allowed** |
| **Cloth Config / YetAnotherConfigLib** | Auxiliary configuration libraries for mod menus | **Allowed** |
| **Fabric API** | Essential Fabric compatibility layer | **Allowed** |

{% hint style="info" %}
If a desired client-side performance mod is not listed, but does not affect gameplay mechanics, lighting, or entity visibility: verify its status with tournament administrators before starting your match.
{% endhint %}

## 3. Forbidden Modifications (Blacklist)

All mods listed below grant an unfair competitive advantage and are **strictly banned**:

| Category | Examples | Reason for Ban |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Minimaps & Radars** | Xaero’s Minimap, JourneyMap, VoxelMap, MapWriter | Displays surface terrain, underground cave networks, entity coordinates, and allows setting waypoints, breaking vanilla navigation integrity. |
| **Lighting Mods (FullBright)** | Gamma Utils, Fullbright, Night Vision, registry gamma hacks | Allows seeing in pitch-black caves and the Nether without torches or potions, granting an unfair advantage in ore discovery. |
| **Wallhacks & ESP** | Freecam, ChestESP, Structure Outlines, Tweakeroo (rendering features) | Highlights dungeon chests, spawners, strongholds, and chunk outlines through solid blocks. |
| **Tooltip Inspection (Jade / WTHIT)** | Jade, WTHIT, What Is That | **Completely banned.** Jade can display exact container contents through block edges, mob health values, and crop growth stages at a distance. Because distance limits cannot be objectively verified on tournament replays, all tooltip inspection mods are prohibited entirely. |
| **Recipe Viewers (JEI / REI / EMI)** | Just Enough Items (JEI), Roughly Enough Items (REI), EMI, NEI | **Completely banned.** Bingo competitions evaluate player memory and mastery of Minecraft crafting recipes. External recipe searching and instant crafting assistance menus are forbidden in ranked play. |
| **Automation & Macros** | Auto-Craft, Macro / Keybind, Baritone, Auto-Fish | All automated keyboard or mouse routines are banned. |

---

## 4. Integrity Enforcement & Penalties

1. **Match Recording:** official tournament participants must record their screen or stream their perspective upon request by referees.
2. **Replay & Log Auditing:** every ranked match is logged server-side. Suspicious actions (such as direct mining trajectories to hidden ores or abnormal crafting speeds) are audited by the referee team.
3. **Penalties:**
   * First offense / refusal to provide match recording upon request: technical forfeiture of the current match.
   * Confirmed use of X-Ray or prohibited cheats: permanent ban from the Bingo Ranked platform, revocation of all seasonal rewards, and disqualification from tournament circuits without right of appeal.
